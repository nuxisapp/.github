
# Nuxis

**A modern insurance business platform, designed to evolve for decades.**

Nuxis is an architecture-first initiative to build a modular, globally adaptable, source-available insurance business platform.

Our ambition is to make sophisticated insurance technology more accessible while challenging the industry's dependence on monolithic systems, costly customization, and restrictive implementation models.

## Our Vision

An insurance technology ecosystem where organizations can adopt, configure, extend, and evolve the business capabilities they need—without being locked into a particular implementation partner or technology stack.

Nuxis is designed for insurers, brokers, agencies, and other participants in the insurance ecosystem, supporting diverse business models, products, jurisdictions, currencies, languages, and distribution channels.

## Architectural Principles

Our foundation is guided by several principles:

- **Domain-driven:** Insurance business capabilities and boundaries drive the architecture.
- **Modular by design:** Organizations can progressively adopt the capabilities they need.
- **Configuration before customization:** Business variation should not require modifying the core.
- **Global foundation, local adaptability:** Jurisdiction-specific requirements are supported without core forks.
- **Explicit contracts:** Modules interact through well-defined, versioned interfaces and events.
- **Temporal integrity:** Historical business states, rules, and decisions remain traceable and reproducible.
- **Security and resilience:** Financial integrity, auditability, and operational reliability are foundational.
- **Long-term evolution:** Upgradeability, compatibility, and extensibility take precedence over short-term implementation speed.

## Stewardship and Ecosystem

Nuxis retains responsibility for product strategy, core architecture, engineering, maintenance, and official releases.

Selected engineering partners may contribute to core development under Nuxis governance.

Organizations remain free to implement, deploy, operate, and support Nuxis independently or through partners of their choice.

Nuxis does not provide customer-specific implementations, production operations, or production incident support.

## Source Availability and Commercial Licensing

Nuxis is pursuing a **source-available commercial licensing model**.

The intended model provides access to source code for evaluation, learning, and development, while requiring commercial licensing for production use.

Active commercial licensing will include eligibility for official core maintenance and releases. Limited run-off rights will protect existing insurance obligations following license expiration, without permitting unrestricted new business.

The licensing framework is under development. Final legal terms will be published before software distribution.

## Current Status

**Phase: Foundation, Governance & Architecture**

We are currently establishing the project's governance, domain architecture, business capabilities, and foundational principles.

Application development will follow architectural validation—not precede it.

Nuxis is not yet available for production use.
